Why Clean Water Matters

Clean water is essential for drinking, sanitation, health, agriculture, and overall quality of life. SDG 6 aims to ensure everyone has access to safe and sustainable water resources. Without clean water, communities face disease, hunger, and environmental damage.

🚱

Water Challenges

Many regions face water scarcity, pollution, and poor water management. Industrial waste, plastic pollution, and lack of proper wastewater treatment reduce the availability of safe water. Climate change also makes droughts more common.

🌍

Solutions

We can protect water by reducing pollution, recycling wastewater, improving water systems, saving water at home, and supporting clean water projects. Communities and governments must work together to ensure a sustainable future.
